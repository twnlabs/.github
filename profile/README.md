## 👋 What is TWN?

TWN Labs is an identity‑native platform for creator‑owned AI Twins. Each twin is anchored to an on‑chain iNFT that binds ownership, entitlements, and verifiable provenance.

- AI twins for identity, presence, and interaction; capabilities expand over time  
- Transparency by design: periodic public reports and verifiable artifacts  
- Secure governance with multisig and safety timelocks
